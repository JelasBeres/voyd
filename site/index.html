01 — Speak
You say one sentence into the dark.
Describe the project the way you'd tell a friend: the token, the vibe, the features. The voyd listens and works out what to build.

      
02 — Descend
It goes deep, and writes everything.
Pages, apps, tools. VOYD writes every file into your folder, down where nobody has to watch it work.

      
03 — Build
It checks before it surfaces.
Preview it, roast it, fix it. And before you trust any token, voyd audit reads the contract from the chain and scores it from 0 to 100.

      
04 — Surface
It comes back live.
voyd ship puts the folder on a real link in seconds. Next on the roadmap: Echo, which keeps your project loud on X while you sleep.
